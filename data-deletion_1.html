Data Deletion Policy

App: DogGO Developer: Anima Ignea Effective: September 1, 2026 Last updated: September 1, 2026
You have the right to delete your DogGO account and all associated personal data at any time. This page explains exactly how to do it, what gets deleted, and how long it takes.

1. Overview

Anima Ignea respects your right to control your personal data. If you decide to stop using DogGO, you can request that we delete your account and all personal information we hold about you, including your dog profiles, walk history, and all other data described in our Privacy Policy.

Account deletion is permanent and irreversible. Once your account is deleted, we cannot recover your data, restore your Biscuits or XP balance, or reconnect you to your previous walk history.

2. How to Request Deletion

You can request deletion of your account and data through any of the following methods:

Option A — Delete directly in the App (fastest)

1
Open your profile
Tap the account button in the top-left corner of the map screen to open your profile overlay.
2
Go to Settings
Scroll to the bottom of your profile and tap Settings.
3
Select "Delete Account"
Tap Delete Account. You will be asked to confirm. Once confirmed, your account deletion is queued immediately.
4
Confirmation
You will receive a confirmation email at your registered address. Data deletion completes within 30 days.

Option B — Request by email

If you cannot access the App, send a deletion request to doggoapp.management@gmail.com from the email address associated with your DogGO account. Include the subject line "Account Deletion Request" and your registered email address or username.

We may ask for additional verification to confirm your identity before processing the request.

⚠️
Deletion is permanent. This action cannot be undone. All your dog profiles, walk history, XP, Biscuits, and purchased items will be permanently removed. Active Premium subscriptions must be cancelled separately through the App Store or Google Play to avoid future charges.
📱
Deleting the app from your device is not the same as deleting your account. If you uninstall DogGO without completing an account deletion request, your data remains on our servers. You must follow the steps above to permanently remove your account and data.

3. What Gets Deleted

The following table describes how each category of your data is handled upon deletion:

Data category Action Timeframe
Account (email, display name, profile photo) Deleted Within 30 days
Dog profiles (name, breed, weight, medications) Deleted Within 30 days
Vaccination records (Premium users) Deleted Within 30 days
Walk history (routes, distances, timestamps) Deleted Within 30 days
XP balance and level Deleted Within 30 days
Biscuits balance and purchased items Deleted Within 30 days
In-app purchase records (financial auditing) Anonymized Within 30 days; transaction records retained up to 7 years (legal obligation)
Encrypted backup copies Deleted Within 90 days of primary deletion
Aggregate and anonymized analytics Retained No personal data; cannot identify you

After deletion, we may retain anonymized, non-identifiable aggregate statistics (e.g., total walks logged in the app) that cannot be used to identify you.

4. Timeline

  • Immediate: Your account is deactivated and you are signed out of the App as soon as you confirm deletion.
  • Within 30 days: All personal data listed above is permanently deleted from our active systems and databases.
  • Within 90 days: Residual copies in encrypted backups are purged as backup cycles expire.
  • Up to 7 years: Anonymized financial transaction records may be retained as required by Chilean tax law (Ley 20.780) and applicable accounting regulations. These records contain no personally identifiable information.

If you submitted a deletion request by email, we will acknowledge your request within 5 business days and complete deletion within 30 days of verification.

5. Partial Deletion and Data Export

5.1 Deleting specific data without closing your account

If you want to remove specific data without deleting your entire account, you can:

  • Remove a dog profile: Go to your profile → tap the dog's name → scroll to the bottom → tap "Delete Dog". This permanently removes that dog's profile and all associated data.
  • Remove your profile photo: Tap your profile photo → select "Remove Photo".
  • Clear walk history: Contact us at doggoapp.management@gmail.com to request deletion of specific walks.

5.2 Exporting your data before deletion

You have the right to receive a copy of your personal data before deletion. To request a data export, email doggoapp.management@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Export Request". We will provide your data in a structured, machine-readable format (JSON) within 30 days.
